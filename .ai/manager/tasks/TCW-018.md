## Field contract

Required real-world evidence:
1. A player is observed shortly before a genuine ESPN lock/availability transition.
2. The deployed site is refreshed while that player is still unlocked/available.
3. The relevant pre-transition lineup/recommendation/availability state is shown.

6. No lock state is manufactured or simulated.
