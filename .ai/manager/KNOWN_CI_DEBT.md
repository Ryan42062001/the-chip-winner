# Known CI Debt

Manager-owned registry for inherited CI failures.

No known inherited CI debt is active.

When needed, create stable entries as `CI-###` with:
- exact failing workflow/job/test identity;
- owning task/lane;
- evidence establishing inheritance;
- last verified SHA/run;
- scope that may rely on the debt;
- closure gate.

Known debt never excuses a new or changed failure.
