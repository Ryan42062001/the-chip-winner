## 4. TCW-048-F01 — HIGH / BLOCKING — Builder checkout controls its own readiness verifier

**Independent adversarial reproduction:** Extracted the frozen runner's `validateCandidate`, `pathAllowed`, and `selectEligibleTasks` bodies and called them on a controlled future Manager-authorized Builder task with valid status/SHAs/PR/branch, `allowed_path_prefixes:['scripts/']`, no helper-specific forbidden paths.
